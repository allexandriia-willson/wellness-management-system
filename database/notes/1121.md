:: New Concept: More Data Types
1. Service Duration = INTEGER
because we do math with it based on the start time.
Eg. Start Time: 2:00 PM
    Duration: 90 Minutes
The system can calculate:
    End Time: 3:30 PM
That's why: service_duration INTEGER
2. Service Price ≠ TEXT
once again, because we do math with it since we're dealing with money.
Eg. Guest Price: $120.00
    Discount:    $20.00
    --------------------
    Total:       $100.00
or
    - Subtotal
    - Tax
    - Tip
    - Provider Commission
    All of which require calculations.
3. REAL
because we're dealing with money & decimals (in most cases)
Eg. 100
    100.00
    99.99
    75.59
INTEGER can only store: 100
                        99
                        75

Business Analysis

Eg. 60-Min Deep Tissue Massage - exists independently.
Whether: 0 people book it
         10 people book it
         10,000 people book it ... the service still exists.
So SERVICES & APPOINTMENT are separate tables.
